Privacy Policy

Temple Collapse · last updated 2026

Temple Collapse is a free-to-play browser game. This page explains, plainly, what data the game collects and how you can opt out. We keep it short and honest.

What we collect

The game uses two analytics services to understand how people play so we can improve it:

  • PostHog — anonymous product analytics (e.g. which levels are started or cleared, where players die, and general usage/session events).
  • CrazyGames SDK — when played on CrazyGames, their SDK handles gameplay events and advertising as described in CrazyGames' own privacy policy.

These services may set cookies or use similar local storage to distinguish sessions. We collect gameplay and usage data only.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not ask for, or knowingly collect, personal information such as your name or email.
  • We do not sell your data.

How to opt out

  • Block cookies or analytics in your browser settings, or use a privacy/ad-blocking extension.
  • Use your browser's "Do Not Track" or private-browsing mode.
  • On CrazyGames, manage your preferences via their consent controls and privacy policy.

If analytics fail to load or are blocked, the game continues to work normally.
